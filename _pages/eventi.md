## Welcome Party 2026
{: #welcome-party-2026}

**9 October 2026 · School of Management and Economics, Turin**

New students met alumni and heard about their experiences after QFI. Three graduates spoke about their work and academic paths, then took questions from students. We also presented our [research on QFI alumni careers](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7582999).

## Welcome Party 2025
{: #welcome-party-2025}

**3 October 2025 · 16:30 · Aula 02, Turin**

Students, alumni and faculty met to welcome the new cohort. Alumni shared their experiences of work after QFI, and the programme included an introduction to the association.
