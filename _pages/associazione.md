The QFI Alumni & Students Association was founded in Turin on <time datetime="2025-10-10">10 October 2025</time> to help people from different QFI cohorts stay connected. The association brings students and alumni together, creates opportunities to talk with faculty and studies where graduates go after completing the programme.

Reunions and welcome parties bring new students and graduates together. Our research on alumni careers explores where a QFI degree can lead, from finance and insurance to academic research.
