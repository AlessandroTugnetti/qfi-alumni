We are QFI students and graduates who want to keep learning from one another. We founded the QFI Alumni & Students Association in Turin on <time datetime="2025-10-10">10 October 2025</time> to give people across different cohorts a way to meet, share their experiences and stay in touch.

At a welcome party or reunion, you might meet someone who has just started QFI, a former classmate or a graduate whose experience helps you think about your own next step. We want to make those conversations easier to have, and to keep them going after graduation.

## What we care about

Our statute puts students’ cultural and personal growth at the centre of the association. That means making room for questions and discussion, helping students organise activities of their own, and keeping a close relationship with alumni and the programme’s faculty.

We are a nonprofit association with a democratic structure, independent of political parties and religious organisations. Members help shape what we do and can bring their own proposals to the association.

## What we do together

We help organise opportunities to get to know each other, including QFI welcome parties and reunions. We also connect students with alumni and employers, so that advice about life after QFI comes from people who can share their own experience.

Our [research on alumni careers](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7582999) is part of that work. By looking at graduates’ first jobs and the paths they take afterwards, we hope to give current students a clearer picture of the choices ahead of them.

If there is something you would like to organise or work on with us, [email us](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it). You can also [find out how to join]({{ '/partecipa/' | relative_url }}).
