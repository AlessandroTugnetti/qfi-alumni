## Students and alumni
{: #studenti}

Whether you are settling into QFI or graduated years ago, there is a place for you in the association. You could help organise the next event, share advice with students or work with other members on a question that interests you.

Current QFI students and QFI graduates pursuing a PhD can apply as **ordinary members**. Alumni can become **honorary members**; ordinary members move to honorary membership when they complete their QFI degree or PhD. The Board reviews membership applications and can help you find the right membership category.

Here is how to get started:

1. **Say hello.** [Email us](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it) or [get in touch on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/5072671/). Tell us a little about your connection to QFI and what you would like to do. We will explain how to apply for membership.
2. **Pay the annual membership fee.** Once your membership is confirmed, pay the **€10 annual fee through [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/qrcodes/managed/d881ef9c-163b-4994-93c1-33edfea58e42?utm_source=payandgetpaid)**. You will also find the QR code [below](#support).
3. **Take part.** Propose a talk or a social event, help organise an activity, or join our [research projects](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7582999). You can bring an idea of your own or [email us](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it) to ask what we are working on.

For alumni, sharing what you have learned since QFI can be especially useful to someone making their first career decisions. You can give a talk, answer questions about your work or further studies, or help students understand what a particular role involves. If your team is hiring, [send us the vacancy by email](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it) and we will circulate it within our selected network of students and alumni.

## Employers
{: #aziende}

If you are looking for people with a background in quantitative finance and insurance, we can help you reach the QFI community. **Employers and alumni can [email us available positions](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it), and we will circulate them within our selected network of students and alumni.** This includes internships, graduate roles and positions for people with professional experience.

Please include the job title, employer, location, a short description of the role and a link or instructions for applying. A closing date and a contact for questions are helpful too.

You can also take part in a discussion about your work, introduce students to your team or suggest a practical project to work on together. [Email us](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it) with what you have in mind, and we can discuss a format that would be useful for students and your organisation.

## Contacts
{: #contacts}

[Email us at associazione.qfi@unito.it](mailto:associazione.qfi@unito.it) with a question, an idea or an opportunity to share.

[Join our LinkedIn group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/5072671/). The group is restricted to students and alumni of QFI and its predecessor programmes.
